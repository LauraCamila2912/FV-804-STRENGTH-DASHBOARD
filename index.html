Decision Rules — Operating Thresholds
On Track Adjust Action Required   Green = on track · Yellow = adjust next asset · Red = 48-hour response

Newsletter

Green Open Rate ≥55%
Yellow 45–54%
Red <45%
Meaning
  • Green — subject line + relevance + audience alignment are healthy
  • Yellow — optimize subject line, timing, or list segmentation
  • Red — likely audience mismatch, weak subject line, or send-timing problem
Next Action
  • Green + strong CTR → repeat subject pattern
  • Green + weak CTR → improve section order / CTA hierarchy
  • Red → fix send logic before next issue

Blogs

Green Engaged Sessions ≥100
Yellow 60–99
Red <60
Meaning
  • Green — topic and title matched reader intent
  • Yellow — topic has interest but weak entry or distribution
  • Red — headline, angle, distribution, or relevance problem
Next Action
  • Green + weak CTA clicks → improve internal CTA placement
  • Yellow → strengthen distribution and title framing
  • Red → revise angle before next blog

Socials

Green IG ER ≥4%
Yellow 2.5–3.9%
Red <2.5%
Meaning
  • Green — content is resonating and building recognition
  • Yellow — content is visible but not sticky enough
  • Red — weak hook, weak format, or low emotional relevance
Next Action
  • Green → repeat theme / format
  • Yellow → improve hook and CTA
  • Red → change content angle, creative format, or posting logic

Workflow Chain — How Each Step Feeds the Next

Step 1
Newsletter
  • Earn the open
  • Earn the click
  • Push traffic into quarter content
→
Step 2
Blog
  • Earn engaged reading
  • Build trust and authority
  • Drive next-step click behavior
→
Step 3
Social
  • Reinforce recognition
  • Expand engagement and shareability
  • Build "this is the gym people talk about" positioning
Step 1 drives traffic into Step 2; Step 2 deepens trust; Step 3 widens recognition and keeps the brand socially alive between conversion moments.

Leadership View

Risks
  • Unsubscribe rate currently higher than preferred
  • Social audience still small, so quality matters more than raw volume
  • Blog success depends on topic-to-reader fit, not just publishing consistency
What to Monitor
  • CTR relative to open rate
  • Profile visits relative to engagement
  • CTA clicks relative to blog engagement depth
  • Whether traffic is assisting consult / trial behavior
What to Optimize First
  • Newsletter section hierarchy and CTA structure
  • Social hooks and save/share value
  • Blog CTA placement and internal linking
  • 30 / 60 / 90-day review consistency
